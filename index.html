Why animated
▶

See the motion

Data flowing, bars filling, states flipping. Motion carries the intuition that static box-and-arrow diagrams can't.

⌘

Real commands

Every lesson pairs the animation with the exact commands you'd actually run, so it's a reference, not just a demo.

◆

Failure-first

Each lesson shows what breaks and why — the dropped SSH, the double charge, the lost row — then how the pattern prevents it.
